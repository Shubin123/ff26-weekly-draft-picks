Does it win drafts?

Rolling-origin backtest: each season is predicted by models trained only on earlier seasons, and every week we run a 12-team draft from all 12 slots. Each strategy drafts against 11 opponents using season-average rankings. Players who don't play score zero, just as in a real league.

Method

Data, old and new. Kaggle: mexwell/ultimate-nfl-database (play-by-play 2013–21, combine), philiphyde1/nfl-stats (weekly player stats 2012–25), tobycrabtree/nfl-scores-and-betting-data (closing spreads and totals), heefjones/nfl-fantasy-data-1970-2024. Live 2026: nflverse box scores, snap counts, injury reports and lines.

No look-ahead. Each candidate player-week only sees games before that week (as-of joins). The candidate pool is everyone active in the last three weeks (plus last year's regulars early on), on their last known team. Players ruled Out are removed, as you'd do on draft day.

Features. Fast and slow EWMAs and season-to-date averages of points, targets, carries, air yards, snap share, target/rush/air-yard share; last season's per-game line; team pass/rush volume; opponent points-allowed ratio by position; Vegas implied team total and spread; age, experience, draft capital, combine speed score; injury designation.

Models. Season average · EWMA · empirical-Bayes shrinkage (method-of-moments σ²/τ² per position) · per-position ridge · LightGBM (with q10/q90 quantile models for floor and ceiling) · XGBoost Tweedie. A per-position non-negative least-squares stack combines them, with weights fit only on earlier seasons' out-of-sample predictions. A logistic model estimates P(plays); projection = P(plays) × points-if-active.

Draft logic. VORP against the replacement level for the league size you pick (12 teams: QB12, RB29, WR30, TE13; scales with team count), positional tiers wherever the projection drops more than 1.25 points, and a snake-draft simulator that fills QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX.
